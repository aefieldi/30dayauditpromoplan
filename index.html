1

Open the board 5 MIN

This plan is for Instagram and TikTok. Thirty short videos, 15 to 60 seconds each, one a day. One video posts to both.

Why short-form

  • Thirty days of daily posts is how you find out which hooks book audits. Short-form is the only place you can test thirty in a month.
  • Every post carries the same caption and the same keyword, so the 30 days compound into one offer, not thirty.
  • Comments are the trigger. A keyword in a comment is a lead you can DM today.

Why not 30 YouTube videos

  • Nobody needs 30 long videos to sell one audit. They need one good one.
  • So YouTube comes at the end: Prompt 6 fills in a masterclass slide deck from your brain dump, you record it once, and it sells the audit for a year.
  • Want more? Take your two best posts from the 30 days and expand each into a 10-minute video.

2

Find 3 to 4 creators 15 MIN

In your niche or next door to it, growing on the platform you picked, posting the kind of videos you'd actually make.

What you're looking for

  • Momentum, not size. A 20K account with videos pulling 300K views teaches you more than a 2M account coasting.
  • Outliers. Scroll their grid and look for the videos with 5 to 10 times their usual views. Those are what Poppy will study.
  • Your format. If you'll be talking to camera, pick talking-to-camera creators. Not a faceless page.
  • Next-door niches count. A business coach's framework works for a love reader. The structure moves, the topic is yours.

Where to look

  • Your own Explore or For You page. The algorithm already knows what you watch.
  • Search the words your people use: "psychic business", "tarot readers", "human design coach".
  • Who the creators you already follow are collaborating with.
  • Copy the profile URL from the share button on their profile, not a single video.

Prompt 2: What's working for them? 5 MIN

Same chat, creators connected. You want frameworks you can run your own topics through, not their posts rewritten.

PROMPT 2 · WHAT'S WORKING FOR THEM?
Now analyze the creator accounts I've added to this board. Look at their top-performing and outlier posts and distill them into repeatable frameworks I can use in my own content to grow my [PLATFORM] account to [GOAL] this year.

For each framework, give me:
- A name for it
- The structure: hook → re-hook (what they say 3 to 5 seconds in to keep you) → body → close
- What they actually say in the meat: how the body is built, what kind of story, analogy or example carries it, and how the CTA lands so it feels like the natural next sentence
- Why it works psychologically
- One example of how I'd use it for [MY NICHE]

Don't give me their posts word for word. Give me the reusable skeleton I can run my own topics through.

Ask for more if it gives you fewer than five frameworks. Six to eight is the sweet spot for a 30-day plan.

Prompt 3: Here's my audit 5 MIN

Same chat, with your brain dump transcript connected. This is the step that turns a growth plan into a booking plan. Don't skip it.

PROMPT 3 · HERE'S MY AUDIT
Now here's the offer this entire content plan exists to sell: my [AUDIT NAME].

I've connected the transcript of me talking it through (plus any audit notes I added). Read it all and give me back, in my own words wherever you can:

1. Who it's for: the person, not the topic. Where they are in their journey right now.
2. What they're experiencing the week before they'd book: the frustration, what they've already tried, what they're saying to themselves.
3. What happens on the call and what I actually look at or tune into.
4. The #1 thing I find blocking them, and the 3 steps they walk away with for the next 90 days.
5. The proof: client results, my own story, specific moments. Flag anything that sounds like a claim I can't back up.
6. The 5 reasons they hesitate to book, and what I'd say to each one.
7. What happens after the audit (the 90-day container), in one sentence.
8. The 10 beliefs someone has to hold before they'd book this. The content has to install these.
9. My one-line pitch for the audit, written three different ways in my voice.

Only use what's in the transcript and notes. If something important is missing, ask me for it before we build the plan.

If it asks you questions, answer them in the chat. Two minutes here saves you rewriting ten posts later.

Prompt 4: Build my 30 days 10 MIN

Same chat. Poppy will take a few minutes and give you a long answer. Read the strategy part first, then the posts.

PROMPT 4 · BUILD MY 30 DAYS
Knowing everything you know about me, my account, what I actually say in my videos, the frameworks we pulled from those creators, and my [AUDIT NAME], build me a content strategy for the next 30 days with ONE job: get the right people to book my audit.

Base it on my outlier content and on the frameworks from the creators I added. Every post should either reach the person my audit is for, shift one of the 10 beliefs, or make booking the obvious next step. Work the 10 beliefs through the month so each one lands at least twice.

Give me the strategy first (what we're doing, why, which beliefs land in which week), then the actual posts for all 30 days.

For each post, give me:
- Day number and funnel stage: TOP (reach the right person), MIDDLE (shift a belief), or BOTTOM (book the audit)
- Which framework it's running
- 3 verbal hook options (the first sentence out of my mouth), each built on a different hook pattern from the list below, in my words
- 1 mid-sentence re-hook: a line I say 3 to 5 seconds in without pausing, as if I forgot to finish the thought, that re-earns attention. Example: "…just kidding, I'm not done, because the thing nobody tells you about [topic] is…"
- 3 title hook options (the text on screen): under 5 words, one word in CAPS, an emoji if it fits, and each one says something DIFFERENT from the verbal hook so the eye and the ear each get a reason to stay
- The outline as exact "say this" bullets, 4 to 6 of them, not a script: what I actually say in the body, in the order I say it, with one story, analogy or example pulled from my videos or my audit brain dump. The last bullet has to set up the CTA so the CTA is the natural next sentence, not a bolt-on
- The verbal CTA: ONE sentence that names what they get and where it is. Never two CTAs in one video
- Caption line 1: "Comment [KEYWORD] when you're ready to [the outcome THIS video promised]". It has to connect to what I just said on camera
- Caption gift line: one line about this video's point, then "comment [GIFT WORD] for [the free thing]"
- Top 5 relevant hashtags for discovery

Hook patterns to build the verbal hooks from (pick what fits the post, never the same pattern two days in a row): confession ("I have a confession to make…") · vent ("Can I vent for a second about…") · "hate to break it to you, but…" · "I don't know who needs to hear this, but…" · "this is why your [thing] isn't working" · "nobody is talking about this" · "the most common misconception about [niche] is…" · "they'll tell you X matters, in reality it's all about Y" · proof ("here's proof you don't need X", then the client story) · "if you were my client with [problem], this is what I'd tell you" · "I wish someone had told me this before…" · "things I had to unlearn to…" · identity callout ("[rising sign / type / role], this one's for you") · versus ("two readers, same gift, one difference") · the question ("so what is the difference between X and Y?") · "put down whatever you're doing and let this sink in"

Rules:
- Every video is 15 to 60 seconds
- Roughly 10 top, 12 middle, 8 bottom. Label each one
- At least once a week: a behind-the-scenes of me preparing for or running an audit, or a client result told as a story. These are my strongest booking posts
- Speak to the person in my brain dump at the level they're actually at. Never describe them as broke, without clients or starting from zero unless my brain dump says that's exactly who the audit is for. Name the plateau, not the poverty
- No income claims as the hook. No urgency I can't back up
- Everything in my voice, using the words and phrases you pulled from my videos

CTA rule, follow it exactly:
In the VIDEO I name the thing and say "the details are below" or "I made this, it's free, grab it below". Never "comment [word]" inside the video itself.
The "comment [KEYWORD]" instruction goes in LINE 1 OF THE CAPTION. That way the exact same video works for organic AND for a paid campaign without re-editing a thing.

If it stops at Day 15, type "continue from Day 16". Then ask it to put the whole plan, strategy and all 30 posts, into one message so you can copy it in one go for Prompt 5.

The caption on every post 10 MIN

Line 1 changes every day. Poppy wrote it. Everything under it is the same for 30 days, so the person who finds you on Day 22 gets the same offer as the person who found you on Day 1.

How it's built

  • Line 1: one line about this video, ending in the comment keyword. From your plan.
  • Your story: two or three sentences, where you were, where you are now, who you work with. Question 13 from your brain dump. The contrast is the hook, not the income.
  • The audit line: who it's for, what they're ready for, what they walk away with, limited availability. Ends with the keyword.
  • The gift line: optional. A second keyword for the wider audience who isn't ready to book yet. Something free you already made.

When someone comments the keyword

  • Set the keyword up in ManyChat (Instagram) so the booking link goes out on its own.
  • No ManyChat? Reply by hand within the hour: "Sent you a DM 🔮" and send the link.
  • Send the booking link, not a "let's chat". They commented because they want the thing.
  • Keep the keyword the same for the whole 30 days. One word, caps, easy to type.

Prompt 6: Your YouTube masterclass ONE AFTERNOON

One long video that sells your audit for a year, built from the same brain dump. The slides are done. You fill them in with one prompt, record once, upload once.

What's in the template

  • 31 slides in the dark look: the promise, who it's for, where they are, your story, the real problem, who they become, your method in three steps with examples, what happens when they book, proof, the invitation, their questions.
  • Every box says what goes in it, in [brackets]. Every slide has speaker notes that say what the slide does and roughly what you say.
  • Three photo frames and three screenshot frames to swap for yours.

How to use it

  1. Open the template in Google Slides below. File, Make a copy, so you're working in your own. (Canva instead? Download the .pptx and use Create a design, Import file.)
  2. In Claude, paste Prompt 6, then under it the breakdown Poppy gave you from Prompt 3 and your brain dump transcript. Send.
  3. Paste the copy into your slides box by box. Right-click each photo frame, Replace image, for your photos. The three screenshot frames are your booking page, your intake form and what they get afterwards.
  4. Record on Zoom or Loom: screen share, camera on, 25 to 40 minutes. The speaker notes are your script. Read them, don't perform them.
  5. Upload to YouTube. Title from the slide 1 promise. Booking link in the first line of the description, your free thing in the second. Pin a comment with the link.
  6. Post it in your community and your stories, and add it to your Work With Me highlight.
