1

Pick your platform and your board 5 MIN

One platform, one plan. You can run it again for the next one.

Short-form board

  • Instagram and TikTok. One plan covers both, you post the same video to each.
  • 30 videos, 15 to 60 seconds each.
  • Pick this if your audience found you through Reels or TikToks.

YouTube board

  • 30 long-form videos with titles, thumbnail text and descriptions.
  • Pick this if you already post weekly on YouTube, or you want searchable content that sells the audit for years.
  • Prompt 4 has a YouTube version. Everything else is the same.

Open the Custom 30-Day Content Plan board in Poppy and duplicate the version for your platform. Your copy is where everything in this guide happens.

2

Find 3 to 4 creators 15 MIN

In your niche or next door to it, growing on the platform you picked, posting the kind of videos you'd actually make.

What you're looking for

  • Momentum, not size. A 20K account with videos pulling 300K views teaches you more than a 2M account coasting.
  • Outliers. Scroll their grid and look for the videos with 5 to 10 times their usual views. Those are what Poppy will study.
  • Your format. If you'll be talking to camera, pick talking-to-camera creators. Not a faceless page.
  • Next-door niches count. A business coach's framework works for a love reader. The structure moves, the topic is yours.

Where to look

  • Your own Explore or For You page. The algorithm already knows what you watch.
  • Search the words your people use: "psychic business", "tarot readers", "human design coach".
  • Who the creators you already follow are collaborating with.
  • Copy the profile URL from the share button on their profile, not a single video.

Short-form board, Instagram and TikTok. Business and content creators, because that's what my audit is about. Yours should be in your lane.

3

Record your audit brain dump 15 MIN

Poppy can only sell what it knows about. Open Loom (or your phone's voice memos), answer these out loud like you're explaining your audit to a friend, and don't polish. Then copy the transcript.

  1. What's your audit called, and is it a reading, an audit, or both?Straight off your Day 1 card.
  2. Who is it for? Describe one real person.Where they are in their journey, what they do, what they already have going for them.
  3. What are they experiencing the week before they'd book?The frustration, what they've already tried, the sentence they keep saying to themselves.
  4. What do they want in the next 90 days? What do they want for real, underneath that?
  5. What happens on the call, start to finish?What you look at, what you tune into, what you ask, how long it is.
  6. What's the #1 thing you usually find blocking them?Patterns from the people you've already helped. Two or three examples.
  7. What do they walk away with?The 3 steps, a recording, a written plan, whatever it is.
  8. What do they send you before the call?The intake form, their numbers, their chart, screenshots.
  9. How do you know this works?Client results, your own story, the moment you first did this for someone. Tell the stories, not the summary.
  10. Why do people hesitate to book, and what do you say to that?Price, time, "I'm not ready", "I've done readings before".
  11. What happens after the audit?Your 90-day container, in a sentence. Price if you want it in the plan.
  12. What does it cost and how do they book?Your booking link. How many spaces you open a week.
  13. Your story in three sentences: where you were, where you are now, who you work with.This becomes the middle of your caption.
  14. What do you never want in your content?Words, vibes, claims, anything that isn't you.

4

Load the board in Poppy 10 MIN

Everything goes on one board and connects to one chat. That's the whole trick: the chat can see your creators, your profile and your audit at the same time.

  1. Open your copy of the board from Step 1.
  2. Paste each creator's profile URL onto the board. Poppy pulls in their videos. Give it a minute per creator.
  3. Paste your own profile URL onto the board the same way. Your last 10 videos and your top 10 are what Prompt 1 reads.
  4. Add your audit brain dump transcript as a text or document node. Add any anonymized audit notes next to it.
  5. Add one chat to the board and connect every node to it: the creators, your profile, your transcript.
  6. Prompts 1, 2, 3 and 4 all go in that same chat, one after the other. Each one builds on the last.

Prompt 1 and Prompt 2 can go in either order. What matters is that both have been answered before you paste Prompt 3.

5

Prompt 1: Who am I? 5 MIN

Paste into the chat with your own profile connected. Read the answer. If it describes someone who isn't you, tell it what's off before moving on.

PROMPT 1 · WHO AM I?
Can you please analyze my last 10 [PLATFORM] videos as well as my top 10 outlier videos to help me determine:

1. My brand style, voice and tone
2. The words and phrases I use a lot
3. What I actually talk about
4. Who my ideal audience is. Be specific about where they are in their journey
5. Looking at the outlier videos specifically: why did these perform best? What was different about the hook, the format, the emotion, or the topic?

I want you to really understand my channel, what I talk about and who I'm talking to, because everything we build after this is based on it.
6

Prompt 2: What's working for them? 5 MIN

Same chat, creators connected. You want frameworks you can run your own topics through, not their posts rewritten.

PROMPT 2 · WHAT'S WORKING FOR THEM?
Now analyze the creator accounts I've added to this board. Look at their top-performing and outlier posts and distill them into repeatable frameworks I can use in my own content to grow my [PLATFORM] account to [GOAL] this year.

For each framework, give me:
- A name for it
- The structure: hook → body → close
- Why it works psychologically
- One example of how I'd use it for [MY NICHE]

Don't give me their posts word for word. Give me the reusable skeleton I can run my own topics through.

Ask for more if it gives you fewer than five frameworks. Six to eight is the sweet spot for a 30-day plan.

7

Prompt 3: Here's my audit 5 MIN

Same chat, with your brain dump transcript connected. This is the step that turns a growth plan into a booking plan. Don't skip it.

PROMPT 3 · HERE'S MY AUDIT
Now here's the offer this entire content plan exists to sell: my [AUDIT NAME].

I've connected the transcript of me talking it through (plus any audit notes I added). Read it all and give me back, in my own words wherever you can:

1. Who it's for: the person, not the topic. Where they are in their journey right now.
2. What they're experiencing the week before they'd book: the frustration, what they've already tried, what they're saying to themselves.
3. What happens on the call and what I actually look at or tune into.
4. The #1 thing I find blocking them, and the 3 steps they walk away with for the next 90 days.
5. The proof: client results, my own story, specific moments. Flag anything that sounds like a claim I can't back up.
6. The 5 reasons they hesitate to book, and what I'd say to each one.
7. What happens after the audit (the 90-day container), in one sentence.
8. The 10 beliefs someone has to hold before they'd book this. The content has to install these.
9. My one-line pitch for the audit, written three different ways in my voice.

Only use what's in the transcript and notes. If something important is missing, ask me for it before we build the plan.

If it asks you questions, answer them in the chat. Two minutes here saves you rewriting ten posts later.

8

Prompt 4: Build my 30 days 10 MIN

Same chat. Pick the version for your board. Poppy will take a few minutes and give you a long answer. Read the strategy part first, then the posts.

PROMPT 4 · BUILD MY 30 DAYS · SHORT-FORM
Knowing everything you know about me, my account, the frameworks we pulled from those creators, and my [AUDIT NAME], build me a content strategy for the next 30 days with ONE job: get the right people to book my audit.

Base it on my outlier content and on the frameworks from the creators I added. Every post should either reach the person my audit is for, shift one of the 10 beliefs, or make booking the obvious next step. Work the 10 beliefs through the month so each one lands at least twice.

Give me the strategy first (what we're doing, why, which beliefs land in which week), then the actual posts for all 30 days.

For each post, give me:
- Day number and funnel stage: TOP (reach the right person), MIDDLE (shift a belief), or BOTTOM (book the audit)
- Which framework it's running
- Verbal hook (what I say out loud in the first 3 seconds)
- Title hook (the text on screen)
- Outline, including the story, analogy or example, and the CTA
- Line 1 of the caption: one line about this video, ending with "comment [KEYWORD]" (the rest of my caption is the same every day, I'll add it myself)
- Top 5 relevant hashtags for discovery

Rules:
- Every video is 15 to 60 seconds
- Roughly 10 top, 12 middle, 8 bottom. Label each one
- At least once a week: a behind-the-scenes of me preparing for or running an audit, or a client result told as a story. These are my strongest booking posts
- No income claims as the hook. No urgency I can't back up
- Everything in my voice, using the words and phrases you pulled from my videos

CTA rule, follow it exactly:
In the VIDEO I say "the details are below" or "I made this, grab it below". Never "comment [word]" inside the video itself.
The "comment [KEYWORD]" instruction goes in LINE 1 OF THE CAPTION. That way the exact same video works for organic AND for a paid campaign without re-editing a thing.

The caption on every post 10 MIN

Line 1 changes every day. Poppy wrote it. Everything under it is the same for 30 days, so the person who finds you on Day 22 gets the same offer as the person who found you on Day 1.

How it's built

  • Line 1: one line about this video, ending in the comment keyword. From your plan.
  • Your story: two or three sentences, where you were, where you are now, who you work with. Question 13 from your brain dump. The contrast is the hook, not the income.
  • The audit line: who it's for, what they're ready for, what they walk away with, limited availability. Ends with the keyword.
  • The gift line: optional. A second keyword for the wider audience who isn't ready to book yet. Something free you already made.

When someone comments the keyword

  • Set the keyword up in ManyChat (Instagram) so the booking link goes out on its own.
  • No ManyChat? Reply by hand within the hour: "Sent you a DM 🔮" and send the link.
  • Send the booking link, not a "let's chat". They commented because they want the thing.
  • Keep the keyword the same for the whole 30 days. One word, caps, easy to type.

11

Post it for 30 days

There's no one right way. There's only your way and consistency.

  • Batch film. Open your page, film Days 1 to 7 in one sitting. Tick Filmed. Captions are already written.
  • Post daily, same time. Copy the caption from the card, paste, post, tick Posted. Two minutes.
  • Answer every keyword comment within the hour. The link, not a conversation.
  • Watch what outperforms. At Day 15, tell Poppy which five posts did best and ask it to rebuild Days 16 to 30 around those. Same chat, it remembers everything.
  • Log your audits. Every booking goes in The Audit Method Daily Tracker, and in the community so we can celebrate it.
  • Day 31: run Prompt 4 again with the new data. Your second plan is better than your first, every time.
